# Credits

Maguire Career Kit was developed by Ruslan Karmannyy from a real career-workspace workflow: connecting an unusual mix of experience to a role, checking the evidence, preparing a tailored draft, and preserving decisions for the next application.

Thank you to Santiago Fernández de Valderrama and the contributors to [career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops), an open-source job-search agent. We studied version 1.35.0 and tested it on a real application while designing Maguire. Their work helped inform this project.

career-ops is a separate project with its own [MIT license](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops/blob/e8332a8ba88c8da1e21eeeeec90bac517807b475/LICENSE). This kit does not bundle the career-ops engine. This acknowledgement describes the influence of that project; it does not establish an affiliation or endorsement.
