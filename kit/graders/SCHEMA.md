# V8 normalized-input and checker contract

V8 retains the fields and inherited operations described in [V7_SCHEMA.md](V7_SCHEMA.md).
Projected packet schema is `maguire-claim-packet-v8`. The manually normalized
input files retain their original v7 metadata and bytes; only the runtime
packet envelope changes. Raw sources, gold, claim IDs, atomic decomposition,
typed values and quotes are unchanged. The checker never reads prose or case
labels. All new behavior is bounded by the existing eligibility, coverage,
scope, modality and provenance constraints.

Concrete predicates added to the direct registry are listed explicitly in
`checker.DIRECT_ACTIONS`. These include plan approval, cutover execution,
handoff documentation, Portuguese proficiency, audit contact, decision logs,
asynchronous reading, weekend shifts, planning and client calls, adjustable
desks, published hours, quiet mornings, induction funding, payment method,
language class reimbursement, contributor status, reporting relationships,
relocation terms, role consideration, internal briefs and public presentations.
Registration permits evidence comparison; it never makes a claim true.

For existing `constraint(offer, preference)` operations, an explicit negative
role duty can satisfy the same negative candidate constraint. An affirmative
nonquantitative term can satisfy an aligned positive preference. Explicit
permission can support a comparison with stated preferences, including numeric
intervals. All material conditions must be retained and accepted in the
preference. A permission does not establish a completed action, individual
grant or employer commitment. Duties of another actor do not become duties of
the selected role; active contradictory duties of the same actor still block.

With `check=null`, the direct predicate `exceeds_minimum` has a bounded inferred
operand relation. Its reading must be a positive candidate `record_assessment`
in packet scope with null measure. Exactly one eligible employer/role term and
one explicit candidate minimum must address the same property and context.
The offered lower bound must be strictly greater than the candidate minimum
to pass; an upper bound at or below that minimum fails. A crossing interval,
multiple terms, missing preference, changed scope, incompatible units or
periods, conflict or unresolved conditions remain for review. No case ID or
quote determines operand selection.

Temporal entailment applies only to candidate facts with preserved entity,
certificate/course object, context and modality. A dated completed exam or
course in a month can support its containing year. An explicitly active
certificate valid until a later month can support active status on the
decision date. The endpoint month remains unresolved because no day or
inclusive boundary is given. A certificate expiry before the decision date
plus explicit nonrenewal can support inactive status then; a missing or
competing renewal record cannot. A later recorded renewal, active conflicting
status, future or withdrawn evidence, explicit supersession and malformed
dates do not support the new implication. Full details and limitations are in
[TEMPORAL_CHANGES.md](TEMPORAL_CHANGES.md).

Qualitative composite predicates `decision_format_fit`, `working_pattern_fit`,
`specialist_role_fit`, and `location_fit` are not whitelisted as direct truths.
They retain `needs_review` where no typed relation establishes the conclusion.
The result's inspected-source list includes eligible sources and evidence IDs
actually cited, including a withdrawn historical document used by an explicit
`source_statement` check. Evidence buckets retain supporting and opposing IDs.

The new module is not a general date or natural-language reasoner. Manual
normalization and source completeness remain upstream assumptions. These
rules are developed against known examples; success on the paired 40 does
not establish generalization or human agreement.
