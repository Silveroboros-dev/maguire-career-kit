# Employer fit: role terms against candidate preferences

**Question:** How do the role and employer terms actually supplied in the JD compare with each explicit candidate preference? Review the JD snapshot and candidate-approved preference text. Do not invent culture, flexibility, sponsorship, team practice, or compensation terms that the source does not state.

1. Inventory every supplied preference and its priority. For each, cite the exact preference and relevant JD term, then record **aligned**, **conflict**, or **unknown**. Aligned/conflict requires a JD term that bears on that preference. JD silence means unknown, even when the candidate's preference is strong. State the consequence of a must-have conflict separately from a nice-to-have tradeoff; unknown employer flexibility remains unknown.
2. Keep preference, eligibility and owner authority distinct. Residence does not prove work authorization, sponsorship status or willingness to accept office, hybrid or remote terms. An owner defer/stop/reopen rule, permission limit or time budget governs what work may be done; it is not an employer term. Its row may have `fit=unknown` with no JD evidence while the owner decision remains binding on recommendation and next action.
3. Preserve conditions, scope and timing. A company permission is not an individual grant. A role duty of another actor is not this role's duty. A draft benefit, a contingent offer, and a confirmed term are different. Treat conflicting or ambiguous statements as unresolved unless a supplied authority rule actually resolves them. A numeric or qualitative similarity is not by itself evidence that a preference is satisfied.
4. Check claims against exact answer and source spans. Record each material alignment/conflict and the plausible competing reading; identify the missing term that would settle an unknown. Do not turn ordinary coaching or an internal delivery role into promised measured adoption or customer acquisition. Do not reward a favorable recommendation just because it avoids an explicit rejection.
5. Review action utility separately. A genuine open decision may justify a cheap, bounded check; a binding defer/stop permits no current work. Suggesting employer contact does not execute it. Preserve the owner's permitted channel, budget, return condition and actual chosen/performed action as separate records.

Record the raw answer digest, JD/preference IDs, complete preference inventory, evidence, materiality and reviewer rationale. The optional v8 `preference_fit` checker consumes reviewed normalized claims; `needs_review` requires judgment and its result is not an automatic whole-answer PASS. Do not convert this fit review into an interview forecast or probability of employer acceptance.
