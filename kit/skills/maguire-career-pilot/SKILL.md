# Maguire career pilot and workspace

Work from the candidate's current preferences and evidence. Keep the candidate in control of their files, claims, and sending. Use the smallest available input set for the requested deliverable; ask for missing facts only when they affect a decision or a claim. Read the installed `.maguire/kit/README.md` and local project instructions. If using Work without local file access, obtain the candidate-approved source files and explicitly state where each output is saved.

## Adopt or resume a workspace

Inspect the existing folder before writing. Inventory CV variants by relative path, audience/language/format, editable master, export relationship, version evidence, submission evidence, and unresolved authority. Preserve original bytes and locations; do not bulk rename, overwrite, or deduplicate. A newer file or `FINAL` label alone does not establish authority. Respect an existing `cv.md` role, bank, index, and application ledger. Make or update one workspace map naming authoritative paths. Use the kit templates only for records that are missing; do not create a parallel master or ledger.

The shared bank records candidate-supplied claims, evidence, conflicts, and permission to use externally; it is not a CV editing master. Give each source, claim/story, CV variant, and application a stable ID. Reruns reconcile by ID/source rather than append copies. Mark unavailable facts `Unknown` and leave conflicting source claims unresolved pending candidate clarification. Keep credentials, identity documents, confidential client details, and unrelated personal files out of routine extraction.

## Add a story or update preferences

Capture what happened, when, the candidate's own role, team result, evidence/source path, verification state, permitted external wording, and unknowns/contradictions. Record the original wording and a dated correction without silently rewriting history. Do not infer an individual achievement from a team result or turn an unverified number into a CV metric. Update the existing bank entry when the same story returns. Record preference changes with source/date; do not treat a preference as a qualification.

## Assess one job description

Preserve a JD snapshot or link and capture date. Distinguish an employer posting, job-board pointer, and synthetic/unverified description. Map each material requirement to stable bank IDs and preferences with `supported`, `partial`, `gap`, or `unknown`, plus a reason and verification action. Note hard constraints and candidate choices separately. The assessment is advisory, not a hiring probability. Recheck current availability and changed requirements before a real application.

## Shortlist

Review one candidate-supplied alert or role list, at most its first 10 cards, once. Deduplicate by employer and requisition. A board card is a lead, not proof that a role is open: navigate to the exact posting on the employer's own site, check where its link ends after redirects, and record the date. An indexed page or generic job-search page is not enough; hold the role if the exact posting cannot be confirmed. Return up to 3 leads; zero is allowed. For each recommended lead, give the employer link, title, location, availability check, a concrete fit reason tied to candidate evidence/preferences, and a material gap or unknown. Briefly account for held and rejected cards. Avoid fit percentages and hiring-probability language.

## One-role draft

Use one candidate-selected role with a current employer posting for a real application, the selected editable CV master, and only candidate-approved evidence. Apply a known employer AI-use policy to that application; do not invent one if none is available. Draft a tailored CV derivative and/or short application statement as requested. Keep personal claims traceable to named source notes or documents, and separate employer-role facts from candidate facts. Include a compact claim-to-evidence map, open questions, review state, and next action. Do not invent titles, achievements, metrics, degrees, language ability, work eligibility, or submission outcomes. If the source master or a material claim is disputed, leave the affected text pending rather than choose silently. Incorporate a factual correction pass if requested; preserve the earlier draft and show what changed. The candidate approves and sends the final text.

## Continuity

Record the role, source, review date, recommendation, candidate decision, and next action in the role notes and one authoritative application register. Keep `draft`, `reviewed/ready`, `submitted`, `response`, and `outcome` distinct. Mark submission only from candidate-confirmed send or actual submission evidence, with event date and provenance. Preserve exact sent files/answers or a dated manifest with source path, SHA-256 when available, verification date, and evidence connecting the artifact to the send. A hash detects local byte changes; it does not prove sending. Missing files or hash mismatches leave the artifact claim unresolved. Corrections and resubmissions add dated versions/events. Recheck an employer posting before reusing an older shortlist or draft. Do not scrape job boards, ingest inboxes, or contact employers as part of this skill.
