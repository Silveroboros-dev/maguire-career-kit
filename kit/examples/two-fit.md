# Worked example of the two fit paths

Everything below is fictional and authored, not a model result, user test or live job. Keep it outside a real candidate bank.

## Supplied facts

- `jd_1`: "Build internal-only AI workflow prototypes with Python and business stakeholders."
- `jd_2`: "Production ownership is desirable, not required. SQL is a plus."
- `jd_3`: "Zurich hybrid, three office days per week. Travel up to 20%."
- `fact_1`: "I personally implemented a Python reference importer with replay and crash-recovery tests. It has no production users."
- `fact_2`: "I led treasury projects and worked directly with finance stakeholders."
- `pref_1`, must-have: "My next role must include direct customer-facing delivery; an internal-only role is outside my current target."
- `pref_2`, must-have: "Three office days in Zurich and up to 20% travel are acceptable."

| Production ownership, desirable | `jd_2`, `fact_1` | unknown | No production evidence; this is not a mandatory exclusion. |
| SQL, a plus | `jd_2` | unknown | No supplied SQL evidence; do not invent either proficiency or inability. |

## Employer and role fit

| Preference | Role evidence | Assessment |

## Decision

Advisory recommendation: decline this role under the supplied current target. The prototype is a real relevant achievement; the hard career-direction conflict determines the recommendation. No interview probability is inferred. The candidate's actual decision remains pending until they choose.

Current next action: none required to apply the stated constraint. If the candidate has already deferred or stopped, preserve that decision. Do not ask them to build another project or investigate culture simply to fill a next-action field.
